WanderCast Privacy Policy

Application Name: WanderCast: AI Walk Audio Tour
Package ID: com.aistudio.wandercast.kxmpzq
Effective Date: November 21, 2026

WanderCast is an AI-powered GPS walking tour and neighborhood radio documentary application built with a local-first privacy architecture. Your completed walk routes, audio documentary transcripts, unlocked digital badges, and app event logs are stored locally on your device inside an Android Room SQLite database.

1. Location Data & Adaptive Low-Battery Mode

  • Real-Time Street Block Detection: When you grant location permissions, WanderCast uses ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION to detect street block crossings and 42m–85m geofence checkpoints, triggering localized 60-second radio episodes.
  • Foreground Walk Service: When background walk mode is enabled, a visible notification ("99.1 FM • WanderCast Walking Guide") runs with a one-tap "Stop Walk" button.
  • Adaptive Battery Saver (<20% Battery): WanderCast monitors device battery level locally via Android's BatteryManager. When battery drops below 20%, GPS polling automatically slows from 4s to 18s (28m displacement) and map tile refreshes throttle to 12s idle intervals.

2. Photos, AI Processing & Zero Broad Storage Permissions

  • Android Photo Picker: WanderCast uses the zero-permission Android system Photo Picker (PickVisualMedia). We never request broad storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES).
  • Firebase AI Logic & Gemini: Street block context, user questions, and user-selected facade photos are transmitted securely over HTTPS to Google Gemini via Firebase AI Logic (protected by Firebase App Check) solely to generate your requested documentary content.

3. On-Device Data Retention & Deletion

  • All saved episodes, walk history routes, digital badges, and optional BYOK API keys reside on your device.
  • You can delete episodes, walk routes, event logs, or API keys at any time inside the app, or erase all data by uninstalling WanderCast.
